04 / BUILT ON OPEN SOURCE

Good type is a shared effort.

Kip Fonts is a collection of independent adaptations by Tony Kipkemboi. Kip Headline builds on Fraunces; Kip Gothic builds on Libre Franklin. The original designers’ work makes this possible.

The fonts are free to use, modify, and share under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. When sharing or self-hosting the font files, include the original copyright and license notices. The complete download includes them.
