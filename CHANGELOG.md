# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-22

- Replaced Kip Headline's original Fraunces cut with the supplied tuned Hahmlet cut.
- Kept the family name `Kip Headline`, nominal weight 700, and TTF/WOFF2 filenames.
- Updated source credits and included the Hahmlet copyright and OFL license.
- Left all three Kip Gothic cuts unchanged.

The new Headline has different metrics and character coverage. Recheck line breaks when upgrading. The versioned v1.0.0 download remains available for projects that need the earlier font.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

Initial release: Fraunces-based Kip Headline Bold and Libre Franklin-based Kip Gothic Light, Medium, and Bold, with desktop fonts, webfonts, CSS, licenses, and source notes.
